---
name: Guided Study product direction
description: User-stated audience, tutoring, account, cost, and visual constraints for the student study app.
---

Guided Study is for high-school and university students. The tutor should guide students with questions and hints instead of handing over final answers. Each student connects their own Google account for study materials. Use Gemini's free quota only, with no paid-model fallback. Keep the interface dark and avoid red and pink.

**Why:** These are product requirements set by the user and should guide future feature and design decisions.

**How to apply:** Preserve these constraints in future changes to the tutor, Google integrations, model selection, and interface. If integrating a service with its own billing, make the cost boundary clear and do not add a paid fallback.
